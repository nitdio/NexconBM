Renovation

Many of our customers renovate their existing home to avoid the cost of buying and moving. It's the ideal option if you want to keep elements of your home and stay in the neighbourhood you love. Contact us for more information.

Knockdown rebuild

A knockdown rebuild means designing, applying for permits, demolition, rebuilding, and inspection — a tedious journey to take on alone. Let us give you a hand: our project management team guides you through the entire process.

Extensions

An extension or home addition adds value to your property and makes the most of the space you already have — an extra floor, bedroom, living room, or garage. Contact us for more information.

Custom design

Multi-unit development

Residential units in Melbourne keep growing in popularity as people look for cost-effective ways to live close to the city. If you're a developer or investor, you need a builder who takes ownership of the project and delivers on time and on budget. How it works
